# MIDI Quality Rules

Before accepting a generated melody, check:

## Musical identity

- Is there a recognizable motif?
- Does it repeat?
- Is there a meaningful variation?
- Can the melody be hummed?

## Rhythm

- Are note durations varied?
- Are there rests?
- Does the rhythm have a recognizable identity?
- Are there intentional syncopations/pickups?

## Phrase structure

- Do phrases have beginnings and endings?
- Are 4/8-bar structures coherent?
- Is there call-and-response?
- Is there a climax and resolution?

## Harmony

- Do important notes relate to the current chord?
- Are dissonances intentional?
- Does the melody resolve convincingly?

## Dynamics

- Is velocity varied by musical function?
- Are climaxes actually stronger?
- Are decorative notes softer?

## Arrangement

- Does each instrument have a different role?
- Is the lead allowed to breathe?
- Does the guitar answer instead of constantly doubling?
- Does the bass support rather than compete?

## Anti-patterns

Reject or regenerate if the result has:

- random note sequences
- no rests
- every bar completely different
- constant note density
- constant velocity
- constant register
- excessive scale runs
- excessive arpeggios
- lead and guitar playing the same melody continuously
- no clear phrase ending
- no recognizable motif

## Final principle

Prefer:

`simple + memorable + developed`

over:

`complex + random + constantly changing`.
