# Genre Rules — Melancholic House × Country

## Identity

The track should feel like an emotional Country/Americana melody placed inside a modern House arrangement.

The fusion should sound intentional, not like two unrelated genres layered together.

## Harmony

Favor:

- minor keys
- relative-major/minor relationships
- minor pentatonic
- major pentatonic for hopeful contrast
- natural minor
- occasional modal color

Useful emotional progressions include:

- i – VI – III – VII
- i – VII – VI – VII
- vi – IV – I – V
- i – iv – VI – V

Do not force these progressions; chord tones must guide the melody.

## Country language

Use selectively:

- pentatonic melodies
- repeated-note hooks
- small melodic ornaments
- expressive leaps
- call-and-response
- guitar-like short phrases
- pickup notes
- descending resolutions

The MIDI does not need literal guitar bends to communicate the style.

## House language

Maintain:

- 4/4 meter
- four-on-the-floor kick
- clear downbeats
- repeated 1–2 bar rhythmic cells
- 4/8/16-bar phrase organization
- gradual energy development

## Fusion principle

Country supplies:

`melody + emotional phrasing + organic motifs`

House supplies:

`groove + repetition + structure + energy`

Neither should dominate completely.

## Emotional target

The default emotional arc is:

`lonely → reflective → hopeful → emotional climax → bittersweet resolution`

Avoid cheerful melodies unless explicitly requested.

## Register

Keep the main lead in a singable range.

Use higher register mainly for:

- phrase climax
- final repetition
- transition
- emotional peak

Do not keep the lead permanently in its highest register.
