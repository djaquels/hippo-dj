# Instrument Roles

## Lead / Vocal-like Synth

Purpose: main emotional melody.

Rules:

- memorable 1–2 bar motif
- medium repetition
- moderate variation
- clear rests
- singable contour
- strongest phrase identity

Pattern:

`A → A → A' → B`

## Country Guitar

Purpose: organic identity and response.

Rules:

- short motifs
- pentatonic language
- answer the lead rather than constantly doubling it
- use pickups
- leave space
- alternate between rhythmic figures and melodic fills

## Bass

Purpose: harmonic and rhythmic foundation.

Rules:

- strongly support the kick
- emphasize chord roots and fifths
- use repeated rhythmic cells
- occasional passing tones
- avoid competing with the lead melody

## Chords / Pads

Purpose: emotional harmonic bed.

Rules:

- simple rhythm
- longer durations
- support the progression
- limited melodic movement
- change voicing/register for sections

## Arpeggio

Purpose: controlled motion.

Rules:

- short repeating pattern
- high rhythmic consistency
- small variation at transitions
- never obscure the lead

## Drums

Purpose: House groove.

Rules:

- kick establishes four-on-the-floor
- snare/clap reinforces backbeat
- hats provide movement
- transitions can increase density
- fills should be short and intentional

Allowed drum names:

`KICK, SNARE, CLAP, HAT, OPEN_HAT, LOW_TOM, MID_TOM, HIGH_TOM, CRASH, RIDE, BONGO, CONGA`
